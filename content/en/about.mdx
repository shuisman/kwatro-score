# About Kwatro Score

Kwatro Score is a free scoreboard for the card game Kwatro. No more pen and paper: tap in the points each turn and the app keeps the standings, shows whose turn it is, and creates a result to share at the end.

This is an independent hobby project. It is **not an official app** and has no connection with the designer or publishers of Kwatro. The name Kwatro is only used to describe which game this app is for. Card drawings and rule explanations are our own.

## Privacy

Player names and scores stay **on your device only**. There is no account and nothing is sent to a server. To see how much the app is used, we anonymously count page visits and started games with GoatCounter, without cookies and without personal data.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq q="Does the app work without internet?">
  Yes. After your first visit everything is on your device and the app also works offline.
</Faq>

<Faq q="How do I add the app to my home screen?">
  On Android you get an “Install” button (or choose “Add to Home screen” in the menu). On iPhone, tap Share in Safari and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I lose my scores?">
  Your data lives in your browser. If you clear your browser data, it’s gone. Safari on iPhone may clean up data of websites you haven’t used for a week; adding the app to your home screen prevents that. Export now and then to be safe.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I correct a score?">
  No. A submitted turn is final, so there’s no discussion. That’s why the button shows exactly what you’re entering (“Anna +52”) and the app asks for confirmation on high scores.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Does the app calculate the doublings?">
  Not yet. You tap in the total turn score, including doublings. The [Kwatro](../kwatro/) page explains how that works.
</Faq>

<Faq q="How many players can play?">
  Kwatro is meant for 2 to 4 players. The app lets you add up to 8.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I move my games to another device?">
  Yes: export a file below and import it on the other device.
</Faq>

<Faq q="What does it cost?">
  Nothing. No ads, no subscription.
</Faq>
